SEARCH & DESTROY COMPANION

SITE STRUCTURE

DEFAULT STARTER EDITION
The normal site address opens the simplified Starter Edition. It is deliberately limited to four sections:
- Learn: eight short Search & Destroy lessons
- Settings: a practical starting baseline with tradeoffs
- Guides: five complete new-account loadouts, each linked to its deeper Full Guide weapon-role group, plus three downloadable archived Season 6 references
- HUD: researched two-thumb, three-finger, and four-finger starting layouts showing manual ADS, 1-tap ADS fire, and hip fire as separate controls

The Starter Edition has no Guided Help, practice tracker, field notes, or large Field Manual. Its purpose is to give a beginner one calm place to start without presenting every tool at once. Its lessons emphasize the unwritten habits that experienced S&D players often learn through trial and error: emotional discipline, useful communication, teammate support, objective-first decisions, and responding to provocation without becoming predictable. Terms and their definitions stay visible at the bottom of each lesson. The Guides section is governed by a fixed content budget: four primary sections, eight lessons, eighteen settings, five loadouts, six HUD patterns, no more than eight primary choices per screen, and compact build-card copy. New depth moves to the Full Guide or replaces weaker Starter material rather than accumulating.

FULL COMPANION
The Full Companion 4.0.0 remains available at:
./full/

A quiet link appears at the bottom of the Starter learning screen. The Full Guide adds Better Judgment for a short post-round check while retaining Guided Help, lesson progress, practice missions, fight corrections, saved pages, notes, the dictionary, the Field Manual, and a searchable 125-firearm Armory. Better Judgment accepts a fragment, names one likely cause without pretending it watched the match, and returns one concrete thing to try next.

Conversation history, saved tips, selected sources, and structured match records are separate local stores with inspect and delete controls. AI is off by default. The browser accepts no provider key; an optional AI request requires an explicitly configured secure server-side or local bridge. Voice listens once only after a button press. Images, clips, and text files are never scanned automatically.

ARMORY METHOD
The Armory is generated from current firearm build/stat pages, then checked as structured data before release. Each normal firearm receives five attachment slots; weapons without a normal Gunsmith, such as the Nail Gun, are identified instead of being given invented parts. Displayed weapon ratings are comparative menu-style values, not physical damage, fire rate, ADS time, or time-to-kill measurements. A before/after rating comparison appears only when the source page's exact attachment list matches the build shown in the Companion. Manual S&D adaptations keep their linked base information but do not inherit unrelated build deltas. Every exact attachment remains subject to confirmation in the live Global Multiplayer Gunsmith because the game can change independently of this static guide.

DESIGN
The Starter Edition uses a dark gunmetal, muted sage, slate, and restrained brass palette intended to feel modern without becoming visually noisy.

HUD METHOD
The Starter HUD diagrams teach control zones and simultaneous finger jobs rather than pretending one exact screen position fits every phone and hand. Official COD Mobile control behavior is separated from community-developed finger patterns. Two-, three-, and four-finger layouts each offer two workflows: manual ADS + hip fire, or those same controls with 1-tap ADS fire added. Every combination includes a short playstyle explanation and its main tradeoff.
The physical baseline is a large 6.7–6.9-inch phone held in landscape, with adjustment guidance for smaller 6.1–6.4-inch phones.
The evidence trail and limits are recorded in HUD_RESEARCH.txt.

ACCESSIBILITY AND SHARING
Both editions include skip links, route-change focus, large mobile controls, reduced-motion support, installable PNG icons, social sharing previews, and a restrictive static-site content security policy.

LANGUAGE RULE
Use ordinary language first. Preserve legitimate Search & Destroy terminology and explain it in context or in the included terms sections. Avoid unexplained social-media slang.

OFFLINE USE
The Starter and Full Companion each use a separate scoped, versioned cache. Updates are downloaded first and applied only when the visible Update control is pressed. The legacy /starter/ service worker removes its old alias cache and redirects to the one canonical Starter.
